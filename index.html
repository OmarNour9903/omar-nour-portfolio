Featured Operations Experience

Executive operational management with company-wide decision-making responsibilities across all departments.

Scope of Responsibilities

Moderation, marketing coordination, design operations, sales, procurement, production (cutting, workshop, engraving, finishing, pressing), shipping, logistics, HR, team management, KPI development, and process development.

Management Approach & Problem Solving

When an operational issue occurs, I understand the root cause, listen to the team, consider customer needs, write a clear process rule, train the responsible person, delegate authority, and follow up until the process becomes stable.

The goal is to build an operation that does not depend on one person for every decision. I develop department leaders so they can handle issues independently. I also research machine problems and document operational manuals so the team can troubleshoot technical issues faster without waiting for external maintenance.

Key Operational Contributions

Expanded overall team structure and added new functional departments.
Built KPI-based accountability frameworks with clear departmental responsibilities.
Improved cross-departmental coordination between sales, production, workshop, and shipping.
Developed department leaders, reducing dependency on direct management intervention.
Accelerated sales-to-production workflows, helping drive business growth.
